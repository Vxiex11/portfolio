About

I'm a computer systems engineering student at ESCOM (IPN) in Mexico City, finishing in 2026. Outside of class I've been doing real security work: cloud and network hardening on GCP and Azure, manual web app pentesting, and lately red teaming AI agents.

Hacking is the part I actually enjoy. But I got tired of finding a way in, writing it up, and walking away. Now when I break something, I want to write the alert that fires the next time someone tries it.

That's why I lean purple team instead of pure offense. Attacking teaches me where things fall apart. Defending is where that knowledge turns into something a team can actually use.
